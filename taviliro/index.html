Designed around everyday routines

Taviliro focuses on organization, reminders, and recordkeeping without making medical decisions for the user.

Medication Organization

Keep medication names, schedules, strengths, directions, quantities, refill information, and optional photos organized.

Routine Reminders

Use Android notifications and scheduling capabilities for reminders associated with routines saved by the user.

Tracking & Journal

Record dose status, hydration entries, meal schedules, and personal wellness observations.

Accessibility

Support for display preferences, high-contrast needs, and optional voice reading using the device's text-to-speech service.

Local-first Privacy

Taviliro V1 does not require a Taviliro account or Taviliro-operated cloud storage.
