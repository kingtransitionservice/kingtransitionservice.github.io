Taviliro

Taking Life Right, One Dose at a Time.

Privacy Policy
Effective Date: August 22, 2026

Taviliro is a personal organization and routine-tracking application designed to help users manage medication routines, schedules, refills, hydration, meals, journal entries, and related personal routine information.

Taviliro is published by King Transition Service LLC.

1. Information Stored in Taviliro

Taviliro may allow you to enter and store information such as:

  • Medication, vitamin, and supplement names
  • Strength, directions, schedule times, and reminder settings
  • Quantity remaining, refill information, pharmacy reference information, and prescription reference numbers
  • Medication photos that you choose to add
  • Dose status and history, such as taken, skipped, missed, or taken late
  • Water-tracking entries
  • Meal schedules and meal-related notes
  • Wellness journal entries and personal observations
  • Appearance, accessibility, notification, and voice-reading preferences

Taviliro Version 1 stores this information locally within the app on your device.

2. Data Collection and Sharing

Taviliro Version 1 is a local-first application. Taviliro does not require a Taviliro account and does not intentionally transmit your medication records, schedules, journal entries, water entries, meal information, refill information, or medication photos to Taviliro-operated servers.

Taviliro Version 1 does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking libraries, cloud synchronization, or Taviliro-operated remote data services.

Taviliro does not sell your personal information.

Taviliro does not use your medication or wellness information for advertising, insurance eligibility, employment decisions, or unauthorized social sharing.

If a future Taviliro version adds an online, cloud, advertising, analytics, pharmacy, caregiver-sharing, or other connected service, this Privacy Policy and the app's Google Play disclosures will be updated before that feature is released.

3. Android Backup

Android may back up eligible Taviliro app data using Android's built-in device backup features when those features are enabled by the user or device.

This backup is provided and controlled by the Android operating system and the user's Google account settings. Taviliro does not operate or control Google's backup service.

Users can manage Android backup behavior through their device and Google account settings.

4. Device Permissions and System Services

Notifications

Taviliro uses Android notification functionality to provide reminders associated with schedules saved by the user. Notification access can be managed through Android Settings.

Alarms and Scheduling

Taviliro may use Android alarm or scheduling capabilities to deliver time-based reminders.

Restart Recovery

Taviliro may receive Android's boot-completed signal so previously saved reminder schedules can be restored after a device restart.

Photos

If you choose to add a medication photo, Taviliro may use Android's photo-selection or media-access functionality so you can choose an image. Taviliro does not require you to add a photo.

Text-to-Speech

Taviliro can use Android's installed text-to-speech service for optional Voice Reading. Text selected for reading is provided to the text-to-speech engine configured on the device. The behavior and privacy practices of a separately installed or device-provided text-to-speech engine are governed by that service's provider.

5. Health and Medication Information

Important: Taviliro is an organization and routine-tracking tool. It does not diagnose medical conditions, prescribe treatment, determine medication dosage, or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

Information entered into Taviliro may be health-related and should be treated as sensitive.

Users are responsible for entering medication and prescription information accurately. Medication instructions should be copied from an official prescription label or entered as provided by a qualified healthcare professional.

6. Local Storage and Deletion

Because Taviliro Version 1 stores its records locally, clearing Taviliro's app storage or uninstalling the app removes locally stored Taviliro application data from that device, subject to any separate Android backup that may exist.

Where Taviliro provides delete controls for individual records, using those controls removes the selected record from Taviliro's local app data according to the app's normal database behavior.

Taviliro Version 1 does not require an online account, so there is no Taviliro cloud account to delete.

7. Security

Taviliro relies on Android's application sandbox and device security protections for locally stored app data. Users should protect access to their device with appropriate Android security features.

Android may encrypt device and backup data using platform security mechanisms. Taviliro does not independently operate a remote storage or transmission system in Version 1.

No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Taviliro is not designed specifically for children. Taviliro does not knowingly operate a service intended to collect children's personal information through Taviliro-operated servers.

9. Third-Party Services

Taviliro Version 1 does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Taviliro may interact with Android platform services such as notifications, alarms, photo selection, text-to-speech, and Android backup.

If third-party SDKs or connected services are added to a future release, their data practices will be disclosed in this Privacy Policy and in the Google Play Data Safety section before release.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Taviliro's features, data practices, legal requirements, or Google Play requirements change. The effective date at the top of this policy will be updated when material changes are published.
