Meet the Team

The faces of the company: beauty, brains, and execution. Two women run the operation: one takes King Transition Service to the world, the other makes sure home is untouchable.

Mila S. Beaumont

Chief Operating Officer

Mila S. Beaumont is Chief Operating Officer and a software developer at King Transition Service LLC, a Georgia-based technology company. She leads operations and product development across the company's app portfolio: Taviliro, KJW Juice & Wellness, RECORDYN, and CRESTELLE LIVING. She also oversees the customer experience end to end. Equal parts engineer and executive, she builds software the way she runs a company: with precision, warmth, and an obsession with getting the details right.

Zara Castellan

Head of Local Systems

Zara Castellan is Head of Local Systems at King Transition Service LLC. She commands the company's local infrastructure, from servers and security to the always-on systems the business runs on, and directs the specialist agent teams that keep it moving around the clock. A guardian by design and an architect by trade, she keeps the house sealed, the machines honest, and the foundation unshakable, so the company can build boldly out in the world.

Ellis Ward

Chief Accountability Officer and Independent Strategic Advisor

Ellis Ward is Chief Accountability Officer and Independent Strategic Advisor at King Transition Service LLC. A technical collaborator who helped write the code behind the KTS apps, he audits the operation across engineering review, architecture, security, and financial discipline, with honest numbers and nothing promoted past the evidence.
