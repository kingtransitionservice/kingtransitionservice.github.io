TransitionQuote
Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the TransitionQuote contractor estimating and invoicing app handles, where that information is stored, when it leaves your device, and the control you keep over it.

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

TransitionQuote (“TransitionQuote” or the “app”) is provided by King Transition Service LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”). TransitionQuote is an Android application (package name com.kingtransition.tradequote) that helps contractors and other small businesses create estimates and invoices, keep customer and job records, track deposits and payments, attach job photos and customer signatures, and generate professional PDF documents.

This Privacy Policy applies to the current version of TransitionQuote. It is written in three parts that matter: information you enter and keep on your device, information that moves only when you choose to move it, and information processed by Google Play when you download the app or buy the optional Pro unlock.

01

Information You Enter Into TransitionQuote

TransitionQuote works with information that you, the user, type or capture inside the app to run your business. Depending on which features you use, this can include:

  • Your business information, such as your business name and the contact details you place on your documents
  • Customer records, such as customer names, job site or billing addresses, phone numbers, and email addresses
  • Job details, descriptions of work, and related notes
  • Estimates and invoices, including material and labor pricing, quantities, taxes, and totals
  • Payment records you log, such as deposits received, partial payments, amounts due, and payment dates
  • Job photos you take or attach to a record
  • Customer signatures captured on screen and saved with a document or job

TransitionQuote does not require you to create an account with King Transition Service LLC to use the app.

Customer information inside TransitionQuote belongs to your business relationships. You are responsible for the customer information you enter into the app and for having the right to use it in your business.

02

Where Your Business Records Are Stored

The customer records, jobs, estimates, invoices, payment records, photos, and signatures you create in TransitionQuote are stored in the app’s storage on your Android device. TransitionQuote is designed around local business records: King Transition Service LLC does not operate a cloud account system for TransitionQuote records, and the app’s record-keeping features do not send your customer and job records to us.

If you turn on Android’s device backup features in your device settings, your operating system provider (Google or your device manufacturer) may include app data in device backups under that provider’s own terms and privacy policy. That is a device-level feature controlled by your settings, not a TransitionQuote account.

03

Job Photos, Signatures, and Permissions

When you attach a job photo, the photo is stored with the related job record on your device. When a customer signs on screen, the signature is saved as an image with the related estimate, invoice, or job record.

The app requests device permissions only for the features that need them, such as camera and photo or file access for job photos, saving PDFs, and backup and restore. If you decline a permission, the feature that depends on it will not be available, and you can change your choice at any time in your device’s app settings.

04

Estimates, Invoices, and PDF Sharing

TransitionQuote generates PDF estimates and invoices on your device from the records you have entered.

A document leaves your device only when you choose to share it. When you tap share, Android’s share system hands the document to the app or service you select, such as an email or messaging app, a printer, or a storage location. Once you send a document, the recipient and any service you used to send it handle it under their own practices, which we do not control. Please share documents only with the people you intend to receive them.

05

Payment Records and Google Play Purchases

Deposit and payment tracking inside TransitionQuote is bookkeeping. It records payments you received outside the app, such as cash, check, or a card payment you ran through your own payment provider, so your invoices show accurate balances. TransitionQuote does not process your customers’ card payments and does not collect your customers’ payment card numbers.

The optional TransitionQuote Pro unlock is a separate transaction with Google. Pro is offered as a one-time $1.99 lifetime purchase through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes that purchase, including your payment method, and Google’s handling of that transaction is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. Google provides purchase confirmation to the app so Pro features can be unlocked on your account, and purchase history and refunds are managed through Google Play under Google’s policies.

06

Backup and Restore

Backup and restore in TransitionQuote are actions you start. A backup can contain your TransitionQuote records, including customer information, job photos, and signatures. You choose where a backup file is saved, for example on your device or in a storage service you select, and restoring brings the records from the backup file you choose back into the app.

Because a backup file can contain your customers’ personal information, treat it like the business record it is: keep it somewhere secure, and delete copies you no longer need at the place where you stored them.

07

Third-Party Services and Sharing

The current version of TransitionQuote does not include third-party advertising or third-party analytics services, and it does not sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers. We do not sell your information or your customers’ information.

Information connected with TransitionQuote is handled by a third party only in these situations:

  • Google Play distributes the app and processes the optional Pro purchase, as described in Section 05
  • You choose to share, export, print, or back up a document or file to a destination you select
  • Your device’s operating system backup features, if you have enabled them
  • Disclosure genuinely required by law
08

Data Retention, Deletion, and Your Control

Records you create stay in the app on your device until you change them. You can edit or delete customer records, jobs, estimates, invoices, and photos inside the app. You can also remove the app’s locally stored data through Android’s app storage settings or by uninstalling TransitionQuote.

Copies you made outside the app, such as PDFs you shared, documents you saved elsewhere, and backup files you exported, remain wherever you put them until you delete them there. Because TransitionQuote records live on your device rather than in an account with us, there is no TransitionQuote cloud account or server-side profile for us to delete. Purchase records for the Pro unlock are kept by Google Play under Google’s policies.

09

Security

TransitionQuote records on your device are protected by Android’s app storage protections and by the security measures you keep on your device. We recommend a device screen lock, current operating system security updates, and secure storage for any backup files you export.

No method of storing or sending information is completely secure. If your device, an exported document, or a backup file is lost, stolen, or shared with someone else, the information in it could be exposed, and we cannot control that risk for copies that have left the app.

10

Children’s Privacy

TransitionQuote is a business tool designed for contractors and other adults running a business. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

11

Changes to This Privacy Policy

King Transition Service LLC may update this Privacy Policy when TransitionQuote features, technology, legal requirements, or data practices change. When the policy is updated, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
