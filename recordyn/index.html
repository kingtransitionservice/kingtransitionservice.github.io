Built for real-world incidents

RECORDYN supports multiple incident types so users can create a clear record without forcing every situation into the same template.

VehicleWorkplacePropertyInjurySecurityPersonalOther

Guided documentation

Capture incident-specific details such as location, people involved, witnesses, conditions, damage, authorities, statements, and supporting notes.

Evidence management

Add photos, videos, documents, and supported voice notes. Evidence records can include identifiers, file metadata, and SHA-256 integrity fingerprints.

Professional records

RECORDYN Pro can generate structured PDF reports and organized evidence packages for user-controlled export and sharing.

Location with accuracy

When the user chooses to capture location, RECORDYN stores the location details together with Android-reported accuracy information.

App lock

Optional biometric or device-credential protection can help keep incident records private on the device.

Local-first privacy

No RECORDYN account or automatic cloud storage is required for incident documentation. Users control when files are exported or shared.

RECORDYN Pro

One-time lifetime unlock.

RECORDYN is free to download. Core incident capture and evidence organization remain available without a subscription. RECORDYN Pro unlocks professional reporting and packaging features through a one-time Google Play purchase.

Privacy & local storage

Incident records and evidence are stored locally on the device by default. Users choose when to capture location, record audio, add media or documents, export reports, or share files.

Important information

RECORDYN helps users organize and preserve incident information. It does not certify evidence, determine fault, provide legal advice, guarantee authenticity, or guarantee that evidence will be accepted by a court, insurer, employer, law-enforcement agency, or other organization.

File-integrity fingerprints are provided to help detect changes. They are not a legal certification or guarantee of admissibility.
