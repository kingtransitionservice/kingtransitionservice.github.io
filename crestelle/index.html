Built for boards, managers, and residents

Crestelle Living connects the whole community in one private ecosystem, with the controls administrators need and the simplicity residents expect.

Roster Import in Minutes

Import the entire neighborhood roster from a CSV or Excel file, with unit numbers, resident names, emails, and household relationships. Keep the list accurate with Active, Pending, and Archived resident statuses.

Secure Invitation Links

Send official invitation links to residents by email. A deep link routes each resident straight into their designated community and verifies their invitation against the roster.

Work Orders with Photos

Residents report community issues with photos and location details, then follow the status as it moves from Open to Resolved. Administrators assign priority and staff, add work notes, and export official PDF work orders for contractors and records.

Official Document Library

Keep bylaws, covenants, meeting minutes, financial statements, and community rules in one central library. Documents are served through signed links that expire after 30 minutes, so files stay protected.

Sub-Admin Permissions

Invite board members and committee chairs as administrators and delegate only the permissions they need: Moderation, Roster, or Content. Roster access never exposes billing or financial details.

Multi-Community Switching

Management companies oversee their entire portfolio under a single login and switch between managed communities in one tap, without logging out.

Feed, Events, and Groups

One unified feed for announcements, events with RSVP tracking, neighbor posts, and community issues, plus public and private group chats for clubs, committees, and streets.

Resident Marketplace

A private marketplace for verified residents of the community only, with categorized listings, photos, and in-app buyer and seller messaging that keeps private phone numbers private.

Privacy Between Communities

Database row-level security keeps every community fully separate. Residents in one community cannot search, view, or interact with the data, chats, marketplace, or residents of another.

How a community gets started

Self-service onboarding, from sign-up to a working community, in three steps.

1. Create the community

An HOA officer or management representative creates an account, chooses the organization type, and enters the community details. The 30-day complimentary trial activates automatically.

2. Import the roster

Add residents one at a time or import the full roster from a CSV or Excel spreadsheet.

3. Invite the residents

Generate and send secure invitation links. Residents tap the link, create or sign in to their account, and land directly inside their community.

Important information

Crestelle Living communities are private. Residents join through an official invitation from their HOA or property management company. If you are a resident and have not received an invitation, check your inbox and spam folder for Crestelle Living, or contact your HOA board and ask to be added to the official roster.
